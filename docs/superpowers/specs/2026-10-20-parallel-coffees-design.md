# Parallel espresso logging

The approved interaction keeps at most two unfinished espresso coffees in one mobile log screen. A sticky, accessible coffee switcher identifies each coffee by its stable session number, bean and recipient. Prepare, Extract and Taste are freely selectable sections, not validation gates. Preparing another coffee copies setup only, takes the selected grinder's latest confirmed household setting, and leaves measurements, outcomes, recipient and media fresh. Saving either coffee returns to the other unfinished coffee; subsequent coffees reuse the free slot without renumbering existing coffees.

Each coffee retains independent controls, recipe/repeat context, section, scroll position and browser-local draft. Drafts are scoped to user, workspace and entry context. Existing single drafts migrate without losing values. File selections survive switching during the page lifetime; recovery after reload asks users to reattach photos. Drafts clear only after server-confirmed success, and failures keep both coffees recoverable. Saving is idempotent per client draft so retries cannot deduct inventory twice. A single-coffee log remains usable without JavaScript.

Confirming preparation records an immutable, workspace-private grinder use, immediately available to other household loggers. The confirmation stores the exact setting text and server-confirmed grinding time separately from Brew save time. Typing, switching coffees and saving an older coffee do not move that confirmation forward. Confirmations are idempotent by a browser-generated UUID. Only workspace writers may confirm use, and bean/grinder references must belong to the active workspace. A confirmed Brew retains its grinding time through export/backup; operational confirmations are included in private export/backup and never public snapshots. Deleting a draft does not undo a physical grinder use.

The latest physical-use reference is per grinder across household members, beans and methods. Compare confirmed grinder uses and saved Brews by actual grinding time, falling back to occurrence time for legacy logs. The selected coffee's historical suggestion remains separate. Never overwrite an existing coffee's entered setting when another use arrives. Preserve strings such as `1/2,50` verbatim; do not parse or round them. A change to bean/grinder/setting after confirmation requires a new confirmation rather than rewriting the previous event.

Timing remains manual. Inventory changes only when an individual Brew saves. Recipe targets, field preferences, machine-dependent controls, workspace isolation and public snapshot privacy retain their existing contracts.
